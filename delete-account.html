Delete your account

Palz Household Organizer · HouseholdHub Software · Last updated 5 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your Palz Household Organizer account, what is deleted when you do, and what is kept.

Deleting from inside the app

This is the fastest route, and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Palz Household Organizer.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Account.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away. There is no waiting period and no further step to complete.

If you have already uninstalled the app

You do not need to reinstall it. Email palzhouseholdhub@gmail.com from the address your account uses, and ask for your account to be deleted.

We will confirm within 30 days, and normally much sooner. Asking from the account's own email address is what lets us verify the request is really yours; if you no longer have access to that address, tell us and we will find another way to confirm it.

What is deleted

DataWhat happens
Your profileDeleted permanently. Your name and your membership of the household.
Your sign-in credentialsDeleted permanently. Your email address and password record.
Your household's reminders and documentsDeleted permanently, if you are the last member. This includes the uploaded files themselves, not only the records describing them.
Pending invitations to your householdDeleted along with the household.

What stays behind, and why

If other people are still in your household, the household's reminders and documents are not deleted. They belong to the group rather than to you, and other members are still relying on them. Your own profile is still deleted, and if you were the owner, ownership passes to the longest-standing remaining member so the household stays manageable.

If you want that shared content removed as well, every member has to delete their account, or you can ask the remaining members to delete the individual items.

Records of subscription payments are kept separately from your account. These are purchase and renewal records: what was bought, when, and whether it renewed. They are kept for up to five years, to handle refunds and disputes and to meet Australian tax and accounting requirements. They are not used for anything else, and no payment card details are held — Google Play processes payments and never passes those to us.

Cancel your subscription separately

Deleting your account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Subscriptions on your device or at play.google.com, or it will keep renewing.
