Privacy Policy

Palz Household Organizer · Last updated 5 November 2026

Palz Household Organizer (“the app”) helps a household keep track of bills, subscriptions, appointments and shared documents. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what control you have over it.

Use of the app is also subject to the Terms of Service.

Who is responsible

The app is operated by the developer of Palz Household Organizer, based in Australia. For any question about this policy or your data, or to exercise any of the rights below, contact palzhouseholdhub@gmail.com.

Where the app is available. Palz Household Organizer is distributed in Australia and a number of other countries. It is not offered in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, and is not directed at people living there.

What the app collects

Email addressTo create and sign in to your account, and to send password reset links.

Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is stored only in hashed form. The developer never sees it.

The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking software. It does not collect your location, contacts, browsing activity, or any advertising identifier.

The app has no camera, gallery or media access, and asks for no storage permission. A file reaches the app only when you pick it yourself through your device’s own file chooser. That file can be a photo, a video or an audio recording as easily as a PDF — photographing a warranty card is a normal way to use the app — so those are listed as data the app collects. The distinction worth understanding is that the app never reaches into your gallery; it only receives what you hand it.

Sharing within your household

This is the most important thing to understand about the app. Reminders and documents belong to a household, not to an individual. Everyone who has joined your household can see, edit and delete them, and can see your name and which items are assigned to you.

Only invite people you intend to share this information with. If you leave a household, the reminders and documents stay with that household, because they belong to the group.

Who else the data reaches

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising. It is processed only by the services needed to run the app:

  • Supabase — hosts the database, authentication and document storage.
  • RevenueCat and Google Play — process subscription purchases and tell the app whether Premium is active. Payment card details are handled entirely by Google Play; the app never receives them.

Where your data is stored

The app is operated from Australia, and the database, authentication and document storage are hosted by Supabase. Depending on the region your data is held in, using the app may involve storing your information outside the country you live in.

Where that happens, our hosting provider is contractually bound to handle it under terms at least as protective as this policy, and the protections described here follow the data wherever it sits.

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Their content is not sent to any server, and disabling them in the app removes them from the device.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account inside the app.

Deleting your account removes your profile and sign-in credentials permanently. If you were the last member of your household, the household’s reminders and documents — including the stored files themselves — are deleted with it. If other members remain, the household’s shared content stays with them, and ownership passes to the longest-standing remaining member.

Records of subscription purchases and renewals are kept separately from your account, for up to five years, to handle refunds and disputes and to meet Australian tax and accounting requirements. They are not used for anything else.

To ask a question about deletion, or to request it by email instead, contact palzhouseholdhub@gmail.com. Full details, including what is deleted and what is kept, are on the account deletion page.

Your rights over your data

Wherever you live, you can ask us to do any of the following:

  • See what we hold about you, and get a copy of it.
  • Correct it if it is wrong. Your name and email can also be changed yourself in the app.
  • Delete it. Settings → Account → Delete account does this immediately, or we can do it for you.
  • Get it in a portable form, so you can take it elsewhere.
  • Restrict or object to a particular use, including anything we do on the basis of legitimate interests.

Write to palzhouseholdhub@gmail.com. We will answer within one month. There is no charge.

One limit is worth being straight about: reminders and documents belong to a household shared with other people. If you ask us to delete content that other members are still relying on, or to hand over a copy of a household's shared documents, we have to weigh your request against their interests too. Your own profile and account are always yours to delete outright.

If you think we have handled your data badly, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to a regulator — in Australia the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au), and elsewhere the data protection authority for the country you live in.

Security

All traffic between the app and its servers is encrypted in transit using TLS. Access to household data is enforced on the server: the database rejects any request for a household you are not a member of, rather than relying on the app to ask nicely.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for their use.
